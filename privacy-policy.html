Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

In short: HabitVerse has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or photos. Your habits, streaks and progress are stored only on your phone. The app uses Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Google AdMob (ads), which collect limited technical data described below.

This Privacy Policy explains how HabitVerse ("the App"), provided by pkora technologies ("we", "us", "our"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or log in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or photos.
  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not store your habit data on our servers.

2. Information stored on your device

The habits you create, your streaks, progress and reminder settings are saved locally on your phone. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. If you clear the App's data or uninstall the App, this data is deleted.

3. Notifications

The App sends reminders so you do not forget your habits. These reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn notifications on or off at any time in your phone's system settings or inside the App.

4. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information automatically:

Firebase Crashlytics (Google) helps us find and fix crashes. It may collect crash logs, device model, operating system version, app version, and an installation identifier.

Google AdMob shows ads in the App. AdMob and its partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which approximate location may be inferred), device information, and ad interaction data. This may be used to show personalised or non-personalised ads, measure ads and prevent fraud.

These services process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase privacy information
  • How AdMob uses data

5. Your choices about ads

  • On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your phone's Settings (Google > Ads).
  • On iOS, you can manage tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

6. How we use information

The limited technical information collected by Crashlytics and AdMob is used to keep the App stable, improve it, and show ads that help keep the App free. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because there are no accounts, we hold no personal habit data about you. To delete your data stored in the App, clear the App's storage in your phone settings or uninstall the App. For data held by Google services such as Crashlytics or AdMob, you can contact us at the email below and we will help with your request as far as we are able, or you can contact Google directly.

8. Security

Your habit data is kept on your device and is protected by your phone's own security. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The App is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has used the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Your rights (India)

We handle information in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You may ask us about the information relating to you, request correction or erasure, or raise a complaint by contacting us at the email below.

11. International users

Google services may process data in countries other than your own. By using the App you understand that such processing may occur under Google's policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.
